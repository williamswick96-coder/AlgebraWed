Core relationships for Chapter 1 (Section 1.6 & Chapter Test):

  • Isolate the Absolute Value First: Before splitting an inequality, isolate $|u|$ on one side. Remember that multiplying or dividing by a negative number reverses the inequality symbol.
  • Disjunction ($|u| > a, \; a > 0$): $u < -a \;\text{ or }\; u > a$, written in interval notation as $(-\infty, -a) \cup (a, \infty)$.
  • Special Cases ($a < 0$): Since $|u| \ge 0$ for all real $u$, an inequality of the form $|u| < \text{negative}$ has No solution, whereas $|u| > \text{negative}$ holds for All real numbers.
  • Slope-Intercept & Point-Slope Forms: $y = mx + b$ and $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$. Parallel lines have the exact same slope $m$.
